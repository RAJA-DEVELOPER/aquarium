Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how AquaVista collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information in compliance with international privacy standards.

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Overview

AquaVista Aquatics Pte Ltd ("AquaVista", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, processing, security, and disclosure when you interact with our website, visit our physical showroom, or purchase live specimens and aquatic supplies.

By accessing our website or purchasing our services, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us as well as data gathered automatically through your use of our platform:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, email address, telephone number, billing address, and shipping address when placing orders or requesting consultations.
  • Payment Information: Credit/debit card numbers processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Stripe & PayPal). We never store raw cardholder details on our servers.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, pages visited, and interaction durations for performance monitoring.
  • Customer Inquiries: Communications submitted via our contact forms, special order requests, or email correspondences.
  • Display Preferences: Local storage settings for your preferred theme (light/dark mode) and text direction (LTR/RTL).
Security Assurance: All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS (256-bit) protocols. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing entities.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for legitimate business and customer service purposes:

  • Processing, packing, and dispatching live fish, aquatic plants, and equipment orders.
  • Issuing essential order confirmations, tracking codes, and live arrival guarantee notifications.
  • Responding to customer enquiries, care consultations, and maintenance plan bookings.
  • Complying with regulatory requirements, including CITES documentation and biosecurity import declarations.
  • Sending weekly digests and new arrival alerts (only with your explicit opt-in consent).
  • Auditing and enhancing platform usability, security, and system performance.

4. Cookies Policy

Our website utilizes standard cookies to deliver a responsive, personalized browsing experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Critical for navigation, shopping cart sessions, and checkout functionality.
  • Preference Cookies: Retain your theme selection (dark/light) and layout preferences across visits.
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymized metrics via Google Analytics to evaluate traffic patterns and page usability.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time, though some interactive features may experience reduced functionality.

5. Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with GDPR, Singapore PDPA, and global privacy frameworks, you retain comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal records we maintain about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request prompt correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and personal records ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction: Restrict processing of your data under specific statutory conditions.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Unsubscribe from marketing newsletters at any moment with one click.
