Our Heritage & Mission

From a 10-Gallon Study to a Living Ocean Sanctuary

Founded in 2010 by marine biologist Dr. Elena Yeo, AquaVista began with a home research tank in Singapore. Driven by scientific discipline and conservation ethics, it has grown into Southeast Asia’s most celebrated destination for rare marine species and master aquascapes.

What We Stand For

Our Mission & Values

Every decision we make — from sourcing to quarantine protocols — is guided by three core principles.

Ocean Conservation

We partner exclusively with sustainable breeders and certified wild-collection operations that operate within strict ecological quotas. 3% of every sale funds coral reef restoration projects.

Scientific Excellence

Every species is quarantined for 21 days, parasite-treated, and health-certified by our in-house marine biology team before reaching your tank. Zero compromise on livestock health.

Community First

From monthly free workshops to our online species database with 2,000+ care sheets — we believe knowledge is the most important accessory in any aquarist's toolkit.

Our Journey

Milestones & Achievements

2010

Founded in Marina Bay

Dr. Elena Yeo opens a 200 sq ft boutique with 50 freshwater species. First week: sold out in 3 days.

2013

Marine Fish Division Launched

Expanded to saltwater species. First CITES-compliant coral frag import in Singapore from sustainable Indo-Pacific sources.

2016

Flagship Showroom Opens

8,000 sq ft three-floor showroom opens with 350 live tanks. Named "Best Aquarium Retailer Asia" by CIPS.

2020

Online Platform & Global Shipping

Launched live fish shipping to 45 countries with 98% arrival guarantee. COVID pivot: virtual tank consultations.

2026

Coral Restoration Partnership

Partnered with Reef Check International — 3% of all revenue funds active reef restoration in the Coral Triangle.
