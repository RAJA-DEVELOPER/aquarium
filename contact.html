Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions about ordering, shipping, and visits.

How quickly will you respond?
All email enquiries are answered within 24 hours on business days. Phone calls are answered during store hours. For urgent livestock health emergencies, call directly at +65 6123 4567.
Do you ship livestock internationally?
Yes, we ship to 45 countries via specialised livestock couriers with 99.2% live arrival guarantee. Minimum order values, CITES permits, and seasonal weather restrictions apply.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), PayPal, bank transfers, and PayNow. A 50% deposit is required for special orders and custom aquarium builds.
What is your return and DOA policy?
Live fish have a strict 48-hour live arrival guarantee. Equipment comes with a 30-day exchange guarantee. Dead On Arrival (DOA) claims require photo evidence within 2 hours of package delivery for full replacement or store credit.
